What TripWatt provides

TripWatt is a manual EV road-trip recordkeeping utility. It stores entries you provide and calculates summaries such as energy use, efficiency, charging power, cost per kWh, totals, trends, and comparisons when the necessary values are available. It also supports user-initiated sharing, CSV spreadsheet export, and CSV backup and restore.
