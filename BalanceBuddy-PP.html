BalanceBuddy - Privacy Policy

BalanceBuddy is a personal-finance application created by Eric Walker. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use BalanceBuddy (the “App”).

Privacy at a Glance

BalanceBuddy is designed to keep your financial information private. The current version processes and stores App data on your device. Eric Walker does not operate a server that receives your financial information.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, tracking, credit decisions, eligibility decisions, or marketing profiles.

Information You Enter or Import

Depending on how you use the App, BalanceBuddy may process information such as:

  • Account names, account types, institution names, balances, and related details you enter
  • Transaction dates, descriptions, merchants, amounts, categories, notes, and display names
  • Budgets, categories, transaction rules, category mappings, and recurring-transaction information
  • Financial information contained in CSV files that you choose to import

BalanceBuddy does not require you to create an account with Eric Walker or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to use the App.

CSV File Imports

When you choose a CSV file, BalanceBuddy temporarily accesses that file so it can read and preview the financial records you selected. CSV processing takes place on your device.

The App may use information from the selected file to create transactions, remember category mappings, identify possible duplicates, and provide the financial features you request. BalanceBuddy does not upload the CSV file or its contents to Eric Walker, and the App does not retain a separate copy of the original CSV file after processing it.

How Information Is Used

Information you enter or import is used only to provide the App’s functionality, including:

  • Tracking accounts, balances, transactions, and net worth
  • Organizing income and expenses by category
  • Creating budgets, summaries, charts, and financial insights
  • Applying transaction and category rules
  • Identifying possible duplicate or recurring transactions

Storage and Apple Backups

BalanceBuddy stores its App records locally on your device using Apple’s data-storage frameworks. The current version does not use CloudKit to synchronize App records between devices.

If device backup or iCloud Backup is enabled, Apple may include BalanceBuddy data in a device backup according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. Eric Walker does not control or have access to those backups.

Data Collection, Sale, and Sharing

The current version of BalanceBuddy does not include third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Eric Walker does not receive your financial records and does not sell, rent, trade, or disclose them to data brokers, advertisers, or other third parties.

BalanceBuddy uses Apple-provided operating-system frameworks for local data storage and user-selected file access. Information handled by Apple through device services or backups is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Retention and Deletion

App records remain on your device until you delete them through available App controls or remove the App. Deleting BalanceBuddy generally removes its locally stored App data from the device, subject to Apple’s backup and restoration behavior.

Because Eric Walker does not maintain BalanceBuddy user accounts or receive your locally stored financial information, he cannot access, retrieve, correct, or delete that information on your behalf.

Security

BalanceBuddy relies on Apple’s platform storage and security protections to help protect locally stored information. You are responsible for protecting your device with an appropriate passcode, installing operating-system updates, and managing your backups. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

BalanceBuddy is not directed to children under 13, and Eric Walker does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BalanceBuddy’s features or information-handling practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are posted. Material new data access or synchronization features will be described in an updated policy before or when those features become available.
