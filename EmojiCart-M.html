A casual shopping game

Emoji Cart

Browse fictional products, spend pretend Cash, track every delivery, and build a colorful collection.

Get support

Browse your way

Explore rotating deals and featured products, or shop eight categories when you have something specific in mind. Choose a variant and add it to your cart.

Follow the delivery

Place simulated orders with free shipping, watch packages move through delivery stages, and check the included widget to see what is arriving next.

Grow a collection

Delivered items join your collection automatically. Track products and variants, level up, complete achievements, and unlock more of the catalog.

Come back to something new

Claim daily bonuses, finish missions, consider rotating buyer requests, open Mystery Packages, and refresh the deals when you choose.

Playful shopping, clearly fictional

Emoji Cart uses fictional products and pretend Cash. It does not order or deliver physical merchandise. Optional consumable Gem packs are available as in-app purchases.

Your game progress and remaining Gems are stored on your device and synchronize through your private Apple iCloud/CloudKit environment when available. No non-Apple analytics or advertising service is included in the reviewed app.
