Using ExpenseKit

Use ExpenseKit to organize receipt records, review recognized text, calculate totals of saved amounts, and create PDF reports and backup files. Check your receipt details and reports

Text recognition can misread a merchant, date, or amount. Review and correct suggestions before saving. Totals depend on saved amounts; the app does not convert currencies. Check for missing and duplicate entries, and inspect PDF previews for omitted or truncated details and image readability. ExpenseKit does not verify reimbursement eligibility, tax treatment, or compliance with an employer’s requirements.

Records and backups

App records are stored on your device; ExpenseKit does not implement CloudKit synchronization. Keep and check a backup before deleting the app, replacing your device, or removing important records. Backup restoration adds missing identifiers and skips existing ones rather than overwriting them. Device backups, file-provider access, and Apple services operate according to their own settings and practices. Availability and recovery cannot be guaranteed.

Reports, backups, and chosen recipients

PDF reports may include collection dates, counts, totals, selected receipt details, and full receipt images or rendered pages from attached PDFs. Hiding a report field does not remove that information from a receipt image. Backup files contain collection and receipt details, identifiers, dates, amounts, categories, notes, images, original PDFs, and combined-charge group information. These exported files are not encrypted by ExpenseKit. Exporting or sharing is your choice. You select where the file goes through the Apple share sheet, including Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email, or another available app or service. A non-Apple recipient you choose may access, store, or retain the copy under its own practices. Deleting the record in ExpenseKit does not delete an exported copy, and ExpenseKit cannot revoke or remotely delete a shared file.
