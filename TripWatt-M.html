EV road-trip records

TripWatt

Log each drive and charging stop, then turn your entries into useful trip totals, charts, and comparisons.

Record the trip your way

Create one-way or round trips, keep multiple vehicle profiles, and enter drive, charging, or zero-mile stops. Add route details, notes, battery percentages, energy, time, and cost.

See the useful numbers

TripWatt calculates energy use, charging power, cost per kWh, average efficiency, trip totals, and cost per distance when your entries provide the needed values.

Review and compare

Explore battery, efficiency, charging, and trend charts. Filter statistics by vehicle, review charging-network totals, or compare two saved trips side by side.

Keep portable records

Share a readable trip summary, export one spreadsheet row per stop, or create and restore a complete CSV backup. Choose miles or kilometers and your supported currency.

Your records, with an iCloud choice

Trip and vehicle data is stored on your device. iCloud sync is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. When enabled, Apple CloudKit synchronizes your records between devices using the same iCloud account. TripWatt contains no advertising, analytics, or non-Apple third-party services.
