Receipt organization for work trips and projects

ExpenseKit

Capture receipts. Review the details. Prepare your expense report.

Keep your receipts together in named collections, track saved expenses, and create a PDF report to share.

Capture your receipts

Scan a paper receipt on a supported device, choose an image from Photos, or import a PDF. Send a supported image or PDF into the app through its share extension, then open ExpenseKit to review it.

Review before saving

On-device text recognition suggests merchant, date, and amount. Select recognized text, correct the details, and add a category and notes.

Keep expenses organized

Search, filter, and sort receipts. Combine related charges while retaining the original entries, or manage categories and move receipts between collections.

See your spending

View collection totals and an all-time overview with category totals, calculated from the amounts you save.

Make the report your own

Choose PDF sections, detail fields, sorting, group presentation, and layout. Preview the summary and receipt pages before sharing.

Back up your collections

Export a backup with receipt records and attachments. Restore a compatible backup using Settings when you need to recover missing entries.

Your records, your sharing choices

ExpenseKit stores its records on the device and recognizes receipt text on the device. No account or subscription is required. It does not implement CloudKit synchronization or automatic non-Apple data uploads. Eric Walker does not sell user information.

Reports and backups can contain sensitive financial details and are not encrypted by the app. You choose where to share them; chosen destinations may retain copies under their own practices.

Read the privacy policy for storage, sharing, and support-email details.

Always check recognized values and the PDF preview before submitting a report. ExpenseKit totals your saved amounts and does not convert currencies.
