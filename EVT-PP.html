EVTools - Privacy Policy

EVTools respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide useful electric-vehicle calculations and tracking tools without collecting, transmitting, or sharing your personal information.

Information Collection and Use

EVTools does not collect personally identifiable information or usage data. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, crash-reporting services, or third-party data-collection services.

Information Stored on Your Device

EVTools may save information you enter or create in the app, where applicable, so its features can work and your preferences can be remembered. This may include:

  • Vehicle battery capacity and energy-efficiency values
  • Battery percentage and charging settings
  • Electricity cost information
  • Charging-session information
  • Other app preferences and settings

This information remains stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or any third party. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting it within the app, where that option is available, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet and Third-Party Services

EVTools does not require an account and does not use third-party cloud services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

EVTools does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

Because information saved by EVTools remains on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including its passcode, operating-system updates, and backups.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as EVTools changes. Updates will be posted on this page and will be effective when posted.
