ChargeBuddy - Privacy Policy

ChargeBuddy respects your privacy. The app is designed to help you log, organize, and understand your EV charging without collecting, transmitting, or sharing your personal information.

Information Collection and Use

ChargeBuddy does not collect personally identifiable information or usage data. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, crash-reporting services, or third-party data-collection services.

Information Stored on Your Device

ChargeBuddy saves information you enter or create so the app can organize your charging history and calculate statistics. This may include:

  • Charging-session dates, times, duration, energy, cost, and charging-speed information
  • Vehicle names and vehicle details used for charging statistics, such as battery capacity
  • Charging locations, charging types, and charging-network names
  • Home and public charging classifications and electricity-cost information
  • Notes, app preferences, and other details you choose to enter

This information remains stored locally on your device. It is used on-device to provide searchable and filterable history, dashboard totals, 30-day and lifetime statistics, monthly trends, charts, comparisons, estimated battery cycles, savings estimates, and charge highlights. It is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

You can remove locally stored records within the app where deletion options are available. Uninstalling ChargeBuddy may remove all locally stored app information.

Internet, Accounts, and Third-Party Services

ChargeBuddy does not require an account and does not use cloud synchronization, third-party cloud services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

ChargeBuddy does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

Because information saved by ChargeBuddy remains on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including its passcode, operating-system updates, and backups.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as ChargeBuddy changes. Updates will be posted on this page and will be effective when posted.
