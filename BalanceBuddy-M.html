Your records, in your iCloud environment

BalanceBuddy stores app data on your device and uses your private iCloud database to synchronize it across eligible devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It does not connect directly to financial institutions or sell user information. Files leave the app only when you choose to import, save, or share them.

BalanceBuddy Pro

Monthly and annual auto-renewable plans provide editing access, with a 14-day introductory trial when eligible and Family Sharing support. Without an active subscription, you can continue to view existing records and use backups, exports, and deletion controls.
