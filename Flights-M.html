A personal flight log

Flights

Record routes, organize trips, and see your flight history through maps and useful statistics.

Get support

Log the details

Keep dates, airlines, flight numbers, airports, flight time, aircraft details, seats, and notes together. Apple Maps search helps identify airports.

Build each trip

Group flights into named, color-coded trips. Reorder an itinerary, add notes, review totals, and explore every leg on a map.

Understand your history

Review lifetime and yearly totals, route maps, airline charts, distance and time, favorite airports, and longest-flight highlights.

Keep and share it

Export JSON backups or CSV records, create shareable trip and yearly cards, and glance at lifetime or current-year totals in widgets.

Your records, in your Apple environment

Flights stores flight and trip records with SwiftData and is configured to synchronize them through Apple's CloudKit service in your iCloud environment when available. Aggregate totals are shared on-device with the app's widgets. There are no ads or third-party analytics.
